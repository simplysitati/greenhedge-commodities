Our Story

A Kenyan company built on quality, traceability, and trust.

We work directly with smallholder farmers and certified estates across Kenya's highlands, ensuring that every product we export meets the most demanding international standards, from field to final destination.

What We Export

Kenya's finest commodities, processed and export-ready.

01

Kenyan Hass Avocado

Sourced from Kenya's highland farms at elevations between 1,400–2,200m, our Hass avocados are prized for their rich flavour, high oil content, and extended shelf life: the preferred choice for European and Middle Eastern buyers.

  • Skin-on & de-stoned variants available
  • Cold chain managed from harvest to port
  • GlobalG.A.P. certified sourcing partners
  • Minimum order: 1 FCL (20-foot container)
Product Details
02

Macadamia Nuts

Kenya is one of the world's top macadamia producers. Our nuts are carefully graded, dried to optimal moisture content, and available in shell, dehusked, and roasted formats, meeting both retail and industrial buyer specifications.

  • Style 0S, 1, 2, 4 & Halves available
  • Moisture content ≤ 1.5% for shelf stability
  • 25kg vacuum-sealed food-grade packaging
  • HACCP-compliant processing facilities
Product Details
03

Kenyan Coffee

Kenyan AA and AB coffee beans are celebrated globally for their bright acidity, full body, and complex berry notes. We source directly from cooperative estates in Nyeri, Kirinyaga, and Murang'a counties, offering washed and natural process options.

  • AA, AB, C & PB grades available
  • Washed & natural process
  • Specialty & commercial grades
  • ICO-compliant documentation provided
Product Details
04

Fine Herbs & Spices

From sun-dried chillies to premium dried herbs, Kenya's equatorial climate produces herbs and spices of exceptional potency and aroma. Our range is air-dried, milled to specification, and packed for both the food service and retail sectors.

  • Coriander, cumin, chilli, turmeric & more
  • Custom grind sizes available
  • BRC/IFS food safety standards met
  • Private label packaging available
