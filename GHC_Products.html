Grown at altitude. Harvested at peak. Delivered to your spec.

Macadamia Nuts

Kenya's premium nut, unmatched in flavour and sought after worldwide.

Kenya is the world's second-largest producer of macadamia nuts, and GHC sources from the Central Highlands where volcanic soils and consistent rainfall produce a kernel prized for its high recovery rate, buttery taste, and natural white colour. Available as whole kernel, halves, or in-shell.

Kenyan Coffee

Bold, bright, complex: the benchmark of East African specialty coffee.

Kenyan coffee is among the most distinctive in the world, celebrated for its bright acidity, full body, and complex berry-citrus flavour notes. GHC sources AA and AB grades from the Nyeri, Kirinyaga, and Embu growing regions — home to Kenya's finest cooperative washing stations. All our lots are cupped and scored before export.
