Who We Are

Connecting Kenya's Farmers to the World's Tables

Green Hedge Commodities Limited is a Nairobi-based agri-processing and export company dedicated to bringing Kenya's finest agricultural produce to international buyers: full traceability, rigorous quality control, and genuine partnerships at every step of the value chain.

We work directly with smallholder farmer networks across Kenya's Central and Rift Valley highlands, providing technical support, fair pricing, and market access that benefits growers and buyers alike. Our products arrive to spec, on time, and compliant with the world's most demanding import standards.

Our Principles

What We Stand For

Four pillars guide everything at Green Hedge, from farm selection to freight.

01
Quality Without Compromise
Every consignment is graded against internationally recognised standards. We do not export produce that falls short. Full stop.
02
Full Traceability
From GPS-mapped farm plots to the final export carton, every product carries a traceable origin. Buyers get full documentation on demand.
03
Farmer Partnership
Fair farm-gate pricing, technical training, and reliable offtake agreements. When farmers thrive, supply chains become truly sustainable.
04
Reliable Trade Terms
We communicate proactively, meet commitments, and handle documentation efficiently. Trust is built shipment by shipment.

Our Founder

Nelson Mandela Ndalila

Founder, Director & Advocate

Nelson founded Green Hedge Commodities bringing a rare combination of legal, financial and governance expertise to the commodities trade. An Advocate of the High Court of Kenya, Fellow of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (FCIArb), Certified Public Accountant (CPA-K) and Certified Secretary (CS-K), he built GHC on a foundation of commercial discipline, sound contracts and principled partnerships.

With an LL.M in Commercial Law and experience spanning legal practice, financial management and institutional governance, Nelson provides the strategic direction and operational rigour that positions GHC as a trusted, compliance-led exporter committed to quality and sustainable growth.

"Our goal is not simply to export. It is to be the most trusted Kenyan exporter our buyers have ever worked with."

Our Journey

Building Towards Global Reach

2018

Company Founded

Green Hedge Commodities Limited incorporated in Nairobi, Kenya, with an initial focus on avocado exports to European buyers.

2022

Farmer Network Established

Formal sourcing partnerships with smallholder cooperatives in Murang'a, Meru, and Nyeri counties, covering over 500 registered growers.

2023

Product Range Expanded

Macadamia and specialty coffee added to the export portfolio, with first shipments to the Middle East and Asia Pacific markets.

2024

Certification Programme

GlobalGAP, KEPHIS, and HACCP certifications secured across key product lines, opening doors to premium retail supply chains in Europe.

2025 –

Scaling for Growth

Investment in processing infrastructure and direct import partnerships with buyers in the EU, UK, UAE, and East Asia. The journey continues.
